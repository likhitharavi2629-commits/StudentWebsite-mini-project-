Introduction

This student performance prediction application allows you to predict how well a student is likely to perform based on the data you enter about their current performance.

  • Attendance percentage
  • Average test score
  • Homework submission percentage
  • Daily study hours
  • Discipline score

Significance

In real-world educational systems, students’ marks are influenced by many variables such as study habits, attendance, family background, previous academic scores, and personal interests. By collecting and analyzing this data, machine learning models can identify patterns and help teachers, parents, and institutions take early action to support students who may be at risk of low performance.

Predicting student performance is useful for:

  • Identifying slow learners early
  • Providing personalized learning plans
  • Improving teaching strategies
  • Reducing dropout rates
  • Enhancing overall academic quality
